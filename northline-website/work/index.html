Selected projects

A few products we designed and helped build. Each one had a job before it had a screen.

  • Keel

    2025

    Harbor operations, Seattle

    A logistics console for coastal freight. Vessels, berths, and weather on one horizon — instead of a forty-tab spreadsheet.

  • Hearth

    2025

    Restaurant group, remote

    A recipe operating system. Mise en place, costing, and service notes that feel like a well-worn notebook.

  • Lumen

    2024

    Personal finance

    A ledger that does not shout. Balances, envelopes, and a yearly view meant for Sunday morning.

  • Atlas Field

    2024

    Field biology

    Mapping for biologists who work in the rain. Offline trails, sample plots, glove-friendly type.

  • Quiet Hours

    2026

    A 200-person studio

    Shared focus blocks without the green-dot anxiety.

  • Folio

    2023

    Literary magazine

    A reading room for long-form on the web. Margins and measure that respect the page.
