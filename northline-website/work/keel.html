Harbor operations, Seattle · 2025

Keel

A logistics console for coastal freight. Vessels, berths, and weather on one horizon — instead of a forty-tab spreadsheet.

Product, interface, build

The job

Dispatchers were piloting the day from spreadsheets, radio, and a weather tab that never matched the tide chart.

What we made

We sat the night shift. The interface became a single horizon: vessels as a timeline, berths as a map, weather as a quiet strip.

After

Shift change dropped from twenty minutes of narration to a glance.
