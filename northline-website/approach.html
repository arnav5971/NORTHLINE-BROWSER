Approach

A small studio, a long brief.

We take a few products a year. The work is slow on purpose: listen, frame, make, and stay. Launch is the middle of the story.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start with the work as it is — the night shift, the pass, the spreadsheet that actually runs the company. Not a workshop. Not a deck.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Constraints become the brief. We write down what the product will refuse to do. Scope is a design material.

  3. 03

    Make

    Design and build in the same room. Interfaces are real before they are presented. Type, motion, and copy land together.

  4. 04

    Steward

    We stay for the tenth year. Products drift; we come back, prune, and keep the original promise intact.

How we work

Engagements usually run three to six months, with a stewarding retainer after. We design and engineer together — no handoff over a wall.

We are not a good fit for growth experiments or marketplace clones. We are a good fit if the product has a job, a voice, and someone who will still be there in five years.
